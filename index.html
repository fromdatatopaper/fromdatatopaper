AI prompt library

Not "write a Results section."
Actually useful prompts.

Every prompt includes what data to fill in, what rules to enforce, and what to avoid asking AI. Replace the brackets — paste — get a strong draft.

RESULTS 
Write a Results paragraph for a scientific paper.

Figure: [DESCRIBE THE FIGURE — e.g. "Bar chart comparing reaction yield
        across 4 temperature conditions (20, 40, 60, 80°C)"]
Main observation: [WHAT THE DATA SHOWS — e.g. "Yield increases from
        23% at 20°C to 71% at 60°C, then drops to 58% at 80°C"]
Statistics: [n=X, p-value, test — e.g. "n=5, p<0.01 for 60°C vs 20°C"]
Control/baseline: [IF APPLICABLE — e.g. "Untreated control: 18%"]

Rules:
- Use past tense. Report specific numbers.
- Do not interpret or speculate.
- Never use "suggests" or "indicates" — that's Discussion.
- One paragraph, 80–120 words.
